Yellow Tape City는 별도의 서버 없이 동작하는 음악 퍼즐 게임입니다. 진행 상황(스토리, 별, 코인, 팔로워, 카드, 설정)은 사용자의 기기 안에만 저장됩니다. 이름, 이메일, 연락처, 정확한 위치는 수집하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

Yellow Tape City runs without its own server; game progress is stored only on your device. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts or precise location. The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase (anonymous usage statistics), Google AdMob (rewarded ads shown only when you choose; uses the advertising ID, which you can reset in Android settings), Google Play Billing (purchases; we never receive payment details) and Google Play Games (scores posted to leaderboards only if you sign in). Friend battles connect two devices directly over WebRTC via the public PeerJS broker; connection info (IP address), room code, nickname and score are exchanged only during the battle and are not stored by us. To delete local data, reset the game or uninstall the app. For analytics or ad data deletion requests, email gkwkdlawoa@gmail.com. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over.
